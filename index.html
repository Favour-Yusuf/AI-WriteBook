What Is AIWriteBook?

AIWriteBook is a faster way to write and publish a book, built for people who have the idea, the knowledge, or the story, but not the six free months.

We do this by turning your idea into an outline, then the outline into full chapters, using your own writing as the reference for tone. You are editing and steering from the first day instead of staring at a blank page.

And as a result, you go from something you keep meaning to start to a polished file you can upload to Amazon, without the parts of writing a book that make most people quit halfway.

Writing a book is harder than it needs to be

The blank page wins more often than it loses

You know what the book is about. Turning that into chapter one, then chapter two, then eleven more, is a different job entirely, and most people never get past the second one.

Your ideas end up scattered across five places

Research in browser tabs, notes in your phone, an outline in a Google Doc you last opened in March, and two draft chapters in a folder you can't remember naming.

The book stops sounding like the same book

By chapter eight the tone has drifted, a character's backstory has quietly changed, and the promise you made in the introduction no longer matches what you're delivering.

Finishing is the part almost nobody reaches

Plenty of people write 30,000 words. Far fewer format an EPUB, source cover art, write the blurb, pick categories, and actually hit publish. That's where the book usually dies.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I get when I buy?

You're buying lifetime access at the founding price. The full workspace opens within 30 days and you'll get an email with your login the day it does. Until then you keep your place, your price, and your refund.

Is the product finished?

No, and I'd rather say so here than have you find out after paying. What you've seen on this page is a working prototype, not mockups. The full version is 30 days out. You're buying at the founding price because you're getting in before it opens, not after.

Do I need to be a writer already?

No. Most people using it have never finished a book. You need to know what the book is about. The tool handles the part where you don't know how to start.

Will the chapters actually sound like me?

That depends on what you feed it. Upload a few thousand words of your own writing and the difference is obvious. Skip that step and you'll get competent, generic prose, same as any other AI tool.

Is fiction supported, or only nonfiction?

Both. The character builder and series support are built for fiction. References and citations are built for nonfiction guides and research-backed books.

What are credits and do they run out?

Credits are used when you generate text or illustrations. They reset every month at the amount shown for your tier and you're never billed again for them.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep access, including future updates to your plan.

What happens after the launch price ends?

AIWriteBook moves to monthly pricing. If you buy during the launch you never pay it, and you keep every feature we add to your tier afterwards.

Will Amazon reject an AI-assisted book?

No. Amazon requires you to disclose AI-generated content when you publish, which takes one dropdown on the KDP upload form. It does not affect whether your book is accepted or how it ranks.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Ask for a refund within 7 days and you get the full amount back. You don't need to give a reason.

Who is this not for?

If you want to write every sentence yourself and enjoy that process, this will get in your way. And if you're looking for a tool that publishes a book without you reading it, please don't buy this one.
